Questions

Dental AI and OPG analysis FAQ

What is dental AI software?

Dental AI software uses artificial intelligence to assist specific parts of dental workflows. In Teta2, AI-assisted analysis helps organize possible findings from OPG and panoramic dental X-rays for clinician review.

Can AI analyze panoramic dental X-rays and OPG images?

Teta2 supports AI-assisted review of OPG and panoramic dental X-rays. The output is not an automatic diagnosis; it is reviewed by the dentist.

Does Teta2 replace the dentist?

No. Teta2 is designed as clinician-controlled decision support. The dentist remains responsible for interpretation, confirmation and treatment decisions.
